Politics: run, campaign, win

The headline: any player can register, join a party, declare for office, campaign, hold rallies and win a seat, all in a fictional Nigeria on real maps.

Get your voter card

Every player can register to vote and collect a voter card. There is no citizenship wait: you register, join the queue, collect the card, and you are in.

  • Register to vote in your own ward
  • Join the card queue (about ten minutes)
  • Collect and share your card

Polling day: cast your vote

When polls open, walk to your ward polling unit, open the voting booth, pick a candidate and confirm. The vote is secret and final.

  • Get a receipt, with the vote itself kept secret

  • Appoint ministers
  • Start programmes: cheaper fares, light, roads
